Terms

Use and content rights

This introduction describes Seen 2.0; the App Store determines which version is available. Seen organizes photos you have the right to use and helps you revisit experiences. It has no public community, messaging service or licensed entertainment catalogue. You are responsible for import and sharing rights and for selecting the intended recipients.

Records and suggestions

After import, Seen organizes experiences using available information. Place corrections, keeping a work and sharing are explicit choices. System recaps and AI suggestions may be incomplete or incorrect; they are not verified visit counts or professional judgments. Missing information is not guessed into facts. AI does not directly modify saved works.

Optional third-party services

Core features do not require a Seen account, subscription or AI. Optional DeepSeek and OpenAI APIs use your configuration and authorization. Provider fees, terms and privacy policies apply independently. Availability depends on networks, providers and account status. Seen does not guarantee fixed quality, costs, retention periods or continuous availability.

Local assets and backups

Core records are local. Asset backup and restore exclude original photos. There is no Seen account cloud sync. Keep the backups you need before deleting or uninstalling. Deleted records have no Recently Deleted page or individual-item recovery. A shared cover is not a complete backup.

System photos and contact

Apple Photos independently manages originals. Only a separate user deletion choice and system confirmation request deletion of specific originals; iCloud Photos may synchronize it. Contact liuh11260@gmail.com for support.
