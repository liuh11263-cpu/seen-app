Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

Scope and developer

This policy covers the Seen 1.0 app and this website, provided by Leo Liu. Last updated: 2026-09-10.

Photos you choose and local experiences

Seen reads photos you actively select and authorize, with available capture dates and locations, to organize material, confirm places and revisit experiences. Original Apple Photos assets remain read-only. Seen does not modify or delete them. Only your explicit confirmation creates an experience. Core experiences, collections and derived results are currently stored mainly on your device. Seen has no account or content-sync server of its own.

Optional AI and DeepSeek

AI is initiated by you. V1 uses BYOK: you configure your own DeepSeek API key. Other providers cannot currently be selected in the app. Before sending, you can review the provider, purpose and scope. Requests go directly to DeepSeek, including your key for authentication, without a Seen-operated server. You may decline AI and keep using all current Free core features.

API key storage

Your key is stored in an iOS Keychain item restricted to this device and not synchronized. Seen does not write it to content JSON, local usage records, ordinary logs or iCloud, and embeds no shared production key. You can update or delete the connection in Settings. Deleting it cannot recall sent requests or erase data already held by the provider.

What AI requests contain

Photo understanding may send reduced JPEG images and necessary Live Photo frames, up to three frames, rather than original photo files, full videos or audio. Images may still reveal personal information. Connection analysis mainly sends dates/capture times, photo counts, confirmed place names/categories, existing visual context and coarse time/distance information for your selected scope. It excludes precise coordinates, detailed addresses and local photo/experience identifiers. Named places may still reveal a specific location. Content depends on the action and scope you explicitly choose.

Third-party processing

DeepSeek processing, retention, use and international transfers are governed by its current terms, privacy policy and your account settings. Seen does not promise that a third party never stores data, never trains on it or follows an unverified fixed retention period. Review the provider policy before sending. AI may be wrong; failures or model outputs do not directly rewrite your confirmed experiences.

Maps and sharing

Map searches, map imagery and navigation use Apple services. Queries or locations are sent to Apple as required by your actions. When you use system sharing, selected photos, text and places are passed to the recipient you choose and are subject to its handling practices.

Usage observation

On-device usage observation records minimal operational metadata such as operation type, status, time, token/resource usage and availability. It excludes source photo content and API keys and is not uploaded as Seen analytics. The provider may separately record calls and charges. Seen has no integrated advertising, cross-app tracking or its own analytics-upload SDK.

Retention, deletion and recovery

Local records generally remain until you delete them. Deleted experiences and collections enter Recently Deleted on this device, without timed automatic clearing in V1. You may restore or confirm permanent deletion. Original system photos are unaffected. Revoking photo access does not automatically remove confirmed experiences. V1 has no in-app cloud sync or full backup/restore. System device backups depend on the operating system and your settings; local storage does not mean every file is excluded from system backup.

Website visits and support

This static GitHub Pages website adds no analytics, ads, tracking scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub as host may process technical visit information such as IP addresses and requests. If you email support, Leo Liu processes your email address and voluntarily supplied content to respond and troubleshoot, retaining it only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. No unverified fixed retention period is promised.

Choices and contact

Manage photo/location permissions in system settings, AI connections in Seen Settings and records in Recently Deleted. Contact Leo Liu about access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. Content held only on your device must be managed there. Direct provider-account requests to that provider. Contact Leo Liu: liuh11260@gmail.com. Share only what is needed to resolve your issue. Do not send API keys, your full photo library, or other sensitive information.
