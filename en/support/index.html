Support

Practical help for your experiences.

Start with photos

Choose Add Photos from Home, select the photos to organize, then check dates, groups and places. Confirming a place is not enough: explicitly save the experience to make it a confirmed record.

Photo access and Limited Photos

You may grant access to selected photos only. To add access, use the photo-permission entry in Seen Settings, or iOS Settings → Apps → Seen → Photos, then return and retry. Denying access does not remove existing records, but source photos require readable access.

Location permission

Location is optional and supports nearby experiences. Denying it does not block core organization or browsing. You can review photo locations and confirm places manually. Maps depend on network and map-service availability.

Past, Footprint and annual records

Past lets you find saved experiences. Footprint shows distributions supported by your available photos and confirmed places. Annual records offer a view over time. Missing locations are not invented.

Themes and saved Seen collections

Themes discover connections in existing experiences; there may be no suitable theme yet. Save a theme to Seen to preserve its presentation, add your own expression, revisit it or share it. Later theme changes do not automatically rewrite your saved collection.

Delete, Recently Deleted and Restore

Deleted experiences and collections move to Recently Deleted on this device. Open Settings → Recently Deleted to restore, or confirm permanent deletion. V1 does not empty it on a timed schedule. These actions never delete original Apple Photos assets. Seen cannot restore a permanently deleted local record.

Unavailable photos

Check that the original exists in Apple Photos and is downloaded to this device, then check Seen photo permissions. Return and retry; use the photo-check or relinking entry in Settings when needed. Do not guess an uncertain match. A temporarily unreadable photo does not automatically remove its experience.

Connect your own AI

Open Settings → AI → Custom AI, enter your DeepSeek API key, save it and test the connection. V1 currently supports DeepSeek only. The key authenticates your requests; DeepSeek charges your account under its own terms. Seen does not supply a shared key or pay these fees.

Connection tests and errors

The connection test checks service access without sending photos or experience content. Check your network, key validity, service access, balance and provider status. Test again after updating the key. Never send a key to support. Removing a connection stops future requests; already-sent requests cannot be recalled.

See Again

Choose the scope, review the sending notice and confirm to start. Photo understanding may send reduced images and necessary Live Photo frames; connection analysis mainly sends structured context. Work can continue after leaving a page while the app process is running. Suspension or force-quitting may interrupt it. Reopen, check status and retry when needed.

Core features work without AI

Photo selection, organization, place confirmation, experiences, Past, Footprint, basic themes, collections, editing, existing sharing and delete/restore remain available. Previously saved valid AI results are not locked when you remove the connection.

Local content and changing devices

V1 has no in-app cloud sync or complete backup/restore. Uninstalling the app, losing a device or changing devices may leave local records unrecoverable. Keep original photos and use existing sharing to retain selected outputs. A shared file is not a full database backup.
