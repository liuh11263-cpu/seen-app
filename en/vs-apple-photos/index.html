Seen and Apple Photos: what is different?

Apple Photos manages your system photo library and offers curated memories and memory movies. Seen lets you explicitly choose a set of photos to revisit by their existing dates and reliable places, then save recaps or discoveries as editable works. You can use both; originals remain managed by Apple Photos.

Choose for your needs

For browsing your full system library and watching memory movies, start with Apple Photos. If you want to choose a set of life records, revisit them by time and place, and keep editable works, explore Seen. If account-based cloud sync is essential, consider that Seen does not currently offer it.
